The Before & After Frame

Every buyer is living in a "before" story. Your copy drags them into the "after" and makes them feel it before they pay. Write the after first. Then show how your product is the bridge.
7:01 AM
Before: Struggling to pay rent every month.
After: Waking up to client payments while Lagos traffic is moving.
7:22 AM ✓✓
✅ The contrast is sharp and real — "Lagos traffic" grounds it
⚠️ "Struggling to pay rent" is too generic — what specific moment does your reader dread?
💡 Try: "Before: Checking your account before buying data."
