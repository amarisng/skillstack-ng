Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

Do I need any prior marketing experience?

No. The Digital Marketing track starts from the absolute basics — what digital marketing is, how Google works, and how to define an audience — before building into advanced campaign management, paid ads, and analytics. If you can read and have a smartphone, you are ready.

How is this different from watching YouTube videos?

YouTube videos are passive. SkillStack is active — every lesson ends with a task you must complete, and you receive personalised AI feedback on your specific submission. The accountability and feedback loop is what drives real skill development, not just knowledge.

What exactly is delivered to my WhatsApp?

Every weekday at your chosen time, you receive a structured lesson with four sections: Context (why this matters), Concept (the actual teaching), Nigerian Example (a real Nigerian business application), and a Task (your practical exercise for the day). You reply with your answer and receive AI feedback immediately.

Can I learn digital marketing and get clients in Nigeria?

Yes — and this track is specifically designed for that outcome. Week 5 and 6 cover pricing your services, client acquisition, proposal writing, client management, and freelancing. The curriculum is built around the Nigerian market with Nigerian examples, clients, platforms, and income benchmarks throughout.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Your lesson waits for you and your day count only advances when you submit your task. If you miss a weekday, you will receive a re-engagement reminder the next morning. Your streak tracks your consistency but missing a day does not reset your progress.

How do I cancel if I need to?

The monthly plan is a Paystack subscription — you can cancel at any time from your Paystack account or by contacting us on WhatsApp. If you cancel, your lessons continue until the end of your current billing period. Your progress is saved permanently so you can pick up exactly where you left off if you return.
