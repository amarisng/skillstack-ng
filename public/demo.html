The Fear Flip

Most Nigerian buyers don't buy because of what they want. They buy because of what they're afraid of losing.

A mother doesn't buy a school lesson plan because she wants her child to learn. She buys because she's afraid her child will fall behind.

The Fear Flip: Find the fear underneath the desire. Write to that fear first. Then show your product as the escape route.

📝 Today's task: Pick any Nigerian product. Write two versions of the opening line — one desire-based, one fear-based. Send both back here.

✅ Strong: The fear version is specific and situational — "client call" creates a real, embarrassing scenario the reader has lived. That is exactly what makes fear copy land.
