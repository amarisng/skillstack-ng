❓ Common Issues & Solutions
My lessons stopped arriving — what do I do?

First check that your subscription is still active — monthly plans renew automatically but can fail if your card expires. Then reply to the SkillStack NG bot with the word LESSON to request a manual resend. If that doesn't work, email support@skillstackng.com with your WhatsApp number and we will investigate within 24 hours.

I paid but never received my first lesson.

This usually happens when the WhatsApp number entered at checkout does not match the number you messaged the bot from. Email support@skillstackng.com with your payment reference and the correct WhatsApp number and we will activate your account manually within a few hours.

I am receiving the wrong track — I paid for one but getting another.

This can happen if you messaged the bot before completing payment. Email support@skillstackng.com with your name, WhatsApp number, and which track you paid for. We will correct it immediately and reset your lessons to Day 1 of the right track.

I want to change my lesson delivery time.

Reply to the SkillStack NG bot with your preferred time — 6AM, 7AM, 8AM, 12PM, 6PM, or 9PM — and we will update your schedule. If the bot doesn't respond, email support@skillstackng.com.

I want to switch to a different track.

Track switches are handled manually to ensure no lessons are lost. Email support@skillstackng.com with your WhatsApp number and the track you want to switch to. We will confirm before making any changes.

How do I cancel my monthly subscription?

Type STOP in the bot chat, or email support@skillstackng.com with your WhatsApp number and a cancellation request. Monthly subscriptions cancelled before your next billing date will not be charged again. Full 90-day subscribers have paid once and have no ongoing subscription.

I submitted my task but got no AI feedback.

Make sure your reply is a proper task response — at least a sentence or two. Very short replies (like just "done" or a single word) may not trigger feedback. Try submitting your full answer again. If the issue persists, email support@skillstackng.com with your WhatsApp number.

I want a refund.

We offer subscription extensions for technical failures on our end (see our guarantee). For refund requests, email support@skillstackng.com within 7 days of your payment with your payment reference. We review every request individually and respond within 48 hours.
