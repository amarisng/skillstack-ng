Common questions

Is there a cost to join?

No. Joining the SkillStack NG affiliate programme is completely free. There are no fees, no minimum requirements, and no approval score. We just want people who will genuinely recommend the product.

How is my referral tracked?

You receive a unique payment link. When someone clicks your link and pays, the sale is automatically tracked to your account. We record every payment made through your link in our system.

When do I get paid?

Payouts happen every Friday for affiliates who have reached the ₦5,000 threshold. We transfer directly to the bank account you provide during signup. You will receive a WhatsApp confirmation when the transfer is made.

Do I earn on renewals?

Yes. You earn ₦500 every month a subscriber you referred renews their monthly plan. A subscriber who stays for 6 months earns you ₦1,500 on the first payment plus ₦500 × 5 months = ₦2,500 in renewals. Total: ₦4,000 from one referral.

Do I need to be a SkillStack NG subscriber?

No — but we strongly recommend it. Affiliates who have gone through the lessons speak more authentically and convert much better than those who have not. If you are not yet a subscriber, consider joining the full 90-day plan for ₦13,000.

Is there a limit to how much I can earn?

No limit at all. Some of our top affiliates will earn hundreds of thousands of naira per month. The more people you refer, the more you earn. There is no cap.
