The problem

Most people who try to learn content writing never finish — or never get paid.

It is not because they cannot write. It is because the training they get does not translate to Nigerian clients, Nigerian platforms, or Nigerian briefs.

📚

Courses teach theory, not briefs

Most courses end at "write engaging content." They never teach you how to handle a real client brief, hit a deadline, or charge what you are worth.

🌍

Foreign examples don't work here

Writing for BuzzFeed or HubSpot is not the same as writing for Zikoko, TechCabal, or a Lagos fintech. Nigerian readers are different. Nigerian brands are different.

🔇

No feedback on actual writing

You can watch 10 hours of "how to write great articles" and still not know if your writing is actually good. You need someone to read it and tell you what is wrong.

Do I need writing experience to join?

No. Day 1 starts with what content writing actually is and why it works. The only requirement is that you show up for 15 minutes every weekday and do the tasks.

How is content writing different from copywriting?

Copywriting is written to sell — ads, landing pages, sales emails. Content writing is written to inform, educate, or build trust — articles, blogs, newsletters, web pages. Both skills complement each other and both pay well in Nigeria.

How quickly can I start earning?

Students who complete the programme and apply the client pitch framework from Week 11 typically land their first client within 30 to 60 days of finishing. Some land clients before finishing. Results depend on how seriously you apply the work.

What if I miss a day?

Your lesson waits for you. Reply CONTINUE any time and your current lesson is sent immediately. The bot also sends a reminder if you miss a day. Life happens — the programme is designed for that.

Can I do this track and copywriting?

Yes. Many students do both — one after the other. Content writing and copywriting are different skills that pay from different clients. Together they make you the most versatile writer in any Nigerian market.
