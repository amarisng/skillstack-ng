Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much do I earn per referral?

You earn 30% of every subscription paid by a student you refer. SkillStack NG costs ₦5,000 per month — so you earn ₦1,500 for every monthly subscription. If a student pays the full 90-day plan (₦13,000), you earn ₦3,900 from that one referral.

When and how do I get paid?

Commissions are calculated monthly and paid directly to your bank account or via transfer. You will receive a payment summary showing exactly how many referrals converted and your total earnings for the month.

Do I need to be a SkillStack subscriber myself?

No — but we strongly recommend it. Ambassadors who are learning on the platform themselves are far more convincing when they talk about it. If you enrol, you can speak from experience, which converts far better than just sharing a link.

Is there a minimum number of referrals required?

No minimum. Whether you refer 2 students or 200, you earn on every single one. There is no pressure and no target you need to hit to stay in the programme.

Can students from any Nigerian institution apply?

Yes. We welcome ambassadors from universities, polytechnics, colleges of education, and any other accredited Nigerian institution. All campuses, all states.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application within 48 hours and send you a WhatsApp message with your approval, your unique referral link, and a short onboarding guide on how to start sharing effectively.
